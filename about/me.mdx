I live in San Francisco now, where I enjoy lots of scenic running, beautiful views and architecture, and trying the city's amazing cafes and restaurants. I'm doing my best to immerse myself in the city: going to free local events and discussions, supporting small businesses, and taking the time to explore and learn about each of the neighborhoods.

## What I believe

I think we should be thoughtful and critical about how we use AI, not just how fast we can adopt it. I believe in stronger regulation and real attention to safety, and I try to bring that same care to how I use these tools in my own work.
