## What I believe

- **Technology should make people's lives better.** I want to build things that help improve people's everyday lives, not things that just enrich the 1%.
- **We should be more critical about AI usage.** Not everything needs to be "AI this AI that." For the love of god we can still use our brains. I believe in stronger regulation and making time for disucssion of safety and privacy.
- **People do their best work when they have balanced lives** and time to do things they enjoy. 996 is ridiculous and it's a no from me. 
- **Get to the point.** I always try to explain things concisely and answer things directly, and like working with people who do the same.
- **Integrity matters.** Be transparent, treat people fairly, and never take advantage of them. I'm drawn to people and teams who hold onto their integrity even when it would be easier not to.
